How to fly

Left thumb
Put it down anywhere on the left and push: left or right to turn, up to climb, down to dive.
Right thumb
Drag to look round your ship. The guns facing where you look are the ones that fire.
The ring
Put it on a raider and it turns red: your gunners lock on and aim ahead of her.
Fire
Hold it to keep firing. Slide your thumb off it to aim as you fire.
Broadsides
Look to her side for your side guns: they hit hardest.
Surge
A burst of speed, to run or to catch her. It fills up again.
Shot
Over Surge, once you have chain shot or crystal breakers: tap it to load the next kind of shot.
Patch
Shows by the sail buttons when she's hurt: the crew patch her up, while the guns reload slower. Its ring fills as they get ready again. They can only patch her so far: the rest waits for the dock.
Red fan
A raider about to fire her broadside: climb out of it and she misses. Diving works about half the time; turning away, only in a small ship.
Sail − / +
Less or more sail: slower or faster.
The map
Tap the corner map to make it big.
