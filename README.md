# Brightvein

A sunstone claim on a plateau under the Thinning, in Aethermoor. Build and staff the town by day, keep the lights
burning through the night when the Wildlight comes, and sail your airship out from the Sky Dock to bring down raiders.

- Play it: https://claude.ai/artifact/XY5KXdzhyr49RD3prE5UP3 (publish `dist/brightvein.artifact.html` here after a change, so this link keeps working and players keep their saves)
- Saves: up to three claims in each browser (Claims, on the title screen). To play the same claim on a phone and a computer, carry it across as a code: Settings › Saving › Carry to another device, then Load from a code on the other one.
- How it's built, and the rules for working on it: `CLAUDE.md`
- One page, everything inside it: `node tools/build.mjs`, then open `dist/brightvein.html`
