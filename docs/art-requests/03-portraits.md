# Portraits for everyone on the claim

In town, people stay pixel sprites. When you tap someone, their card shows their portrait, and so do the story scenes. These prompts are for those portraits.

**Every settler on the claim comes from this list,** with the same name and looks as their portrait, so the sprite walking round town and the picture always match. (This file is written by `tools/portrait-prompts.mjs` from `src/claim/folk.js`. To change someone, edit them there and run the tool again.)

## How to make them

- Make each picture square, at least 1024 × 1024. The game shrinks them to 256 × 256.
- Save each as the file name given (PNG or WebP) and send them to me in the chat, a few at a time or all at once. They go in `assets/portraits/`.
- Anyone without a picture yet gets a bust drawn from their sprite, so there's no need to do them all at once. The six founders and the Baron come first.
- The ten visitors (Tamsin, Brisa, Dame Seraphine, Barnabus, Nib, Torvald and the Moon Faire's four) already have portraits, borrowed from your other games, so they aren't here.

## The style, for every picture

Paste this in front of each prompt below:

> Detailed HD pixel-art character portrait, in the same style as the visitors' portraits already in the game (assets/cast): crisp high-detail pixel art like a modern JRPG's dialogue portrait, a head-and-shoulders bust facing the viewer with a slight three-quarter turn, large expressive eyes with bright highlights, rich soft shading with plum-tinted shadows and warm cream highlights, dark coloured outlines (not pure black), gentle warm light from the upper left. A steampunk-solarpunk frontier in a world of airships and amber sunstone crystal: brass, canvas, leather, wool, small amber crystal details. Transparent background (or a flat dark plum, #2a1b2f, if transparency isn't possible). No text, no frame, no border. Square.
