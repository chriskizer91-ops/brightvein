    How to play
    New claim

    Start a new claim? Your town and people here will be lost.

    Progress is saved in this browser: the claim, your people and your fleet. Training and expeditions keep going while you're away (up to 8 hours).

    A claim in Aethermoor

    Brightvein Keep

    A seam of raw sunstone has broken the surface on a high plateau under the Thinning. Your crew got here first. Dig the vein, charge the crystal in the sun, and keep the lights burning through the night, when the Wildlight wakes.

    • Lay out the claim. Drag buildings around the grid. Where you put the yards and turrets is your defence.
    • By day miners dig ore. Tenders lay it out in the sunning yards, where it charges into shards.
    • By night shards light the town and power the turrets. Wildlight comes for the glow.
    • Inside the Keep your people study: a room for each of Grit, Engineering, Agility, Rapport and Sowing. Time keeps counting while you're away.
    • Tap anyone to give them a job, send them to study, kit them out, or send them down the cliff into the wilds.
    • At the Sky Dock your fighting ship sets sail over Aethermoor to bring down raiders. Their shards come home to the claim.
