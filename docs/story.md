# Brightvein's story

Brightvein's own story, told in chapters (the code is `src/claim/story.js`). Each chapter opens with a short scene, sets two or three goals you can see in the journal (top left; tap it for the whole story so far and the codex), and closes with a scene and a reward. Scenes pause the claim; tap to go on, or Skip.

Characters from Chris's other games visit and can stay as crew. They keep their own story: Brightvein doesn't retell Envoi or the Moon Faire. Their own games' lore comes first.

## The premise

A seam of raw sunstone breaks the surface on a high plateau in the Ironspire Peaks, just under the Thinning. Six founders get there first: Hesper Lark (captain of the courier Lark), Odile Fenn (lens grinder), Pip Kettleby (night lookout), Marigold Thresh (gardener and beekeeper), Corwin Ashlar (wallwright) and Absalom Pockett (the gnome assayer).

Two things want the claim's light:

- **The Wildlight**: loose sunstone light that climbs the cliffs every night and drinks whatever's brighter than itself.
- **Baron Ambrose Tallow**, who made his fortune in candles and lamp oil. Every sunstone claim that opens puts his wicks out of work, so he jumps them. His crews fly rust-red sails; his citadel is the Chandlery, a candle-works on a lifted rock.

## The cast as crew

While they're on the claim (not away in the wilds), each brings a gift to everyone:

| | Gift |
|---|---|
| Io | Moonlore: the lamps burn a third less charge at night |
| Sol | Warden's drill: everyone on the watch hits a quarter harder |
| Quill | Old skyman: voyages bring home a tenth more shards |
| Tock | Gnome mirrors: the sunning yards charge a fifth faster |
| Brann | Smith's edge: every weapon on the claim hits a tenth harder |
