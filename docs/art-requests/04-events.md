# Pictures for the days on the claim

Now and then, by day, something happens on the claim: a courier with a rare trade, a fever, a festival, a lost
Wildlight cub, a wedding, a sky-whale passing under the rim. Each one comes up as a small card with a choice to make
(`src/claim/events.js`). These prompts are for the picture at the top of each card. Until a card has a picture, it
shows the faces of the people in it instead, so there's no need to do them all at once.

## How to make them

- Make each picture landscape, 4:3, at least 1024 × 768. The game shrinks them to 480 × 360.
- Save each as the file name given (PNG or WebP) and send them to me in the chat, a few at a time or all at once.
  They go in `assets/events/`, and the build picks them up by name.
- The cards that happen most come first: the courier, the fever, the festival, the wedding, the quarrel and the bees.
- If you still have the old asset pack (`brightvein-assets.zip`), three of its painted event pictures fit already:
  `crate-cracks` for the rattling crate, `node-overheats` for the hot yards, and `shard-glows` for the singing shard.
  Send them along and I'll use them.

## The style, for every picture

Paste this in front of each prompt below:

> Detailed HD pixel-art scene illustration, like an event picture in a modern JRPG: crisp high-detail pixel art,
> a small scene seen from a little above, rich soft shading with plum-tinted shadows and warm cream highlights, dark
> coloured outlines (not pure black), warm light from the upper left. The setting is Brightvein, a sunstone mining
> claim on a high plateau above a sea of cloud, in a steampunk-solarpunk world of airships and amber sunstone crystal:
> brass, timber, canvas, slate roofs, glowing amber crystals laid out on trays in the sun. Chibi-proportioned people
> like the game's pixel sprites. No text, no frame, no border. Landscape, 4:3.

## The pictures

### A courier with a rare trade
File: `courier.png`

> A small brass courier airship moored at a tall mast at the edge of the plateau. A gnome courier in a flying cap and
> goggles sits on a wooden crate, arms folded, while an elderly gnome in round spectacles and a tall red hat peers
> into the open crate, where something wrapped in oilcloth glints.

### A prospector at the gate
File: `prospector.png`

> A weathered prospector walking up a long wooden boarding bridge toward a stone wall, a pick over one shoulder and a
> heavy pack on their back, dust on their boots, with the sea of cloud far below the bridge and the claim's lamps
> ahead.

### A fever in the Keep
File: `fever.png`

> Inside a cosy stone dormitory with bunks and a stove: a young settler tucked up in bed with flushed cheeks, a damp
> cloth on their forehead, while a gardener in a straw sun hat holds out a steaming mug of honey tonic. Jars of herbs
> on a shelf.

### A festival day
File: `festival.png`

> Lanterns strung between sunning yards full of glowing amber trays, a fiddler on an upturned barrel, people dancing
> on the lawn, a long table heaped with pies, bunting in plum and gold, afternoon sun.

### A lost Wildlight cub
File: `cub.png`

> A tiny creature made of loose amber light, no bigger than a cat, curled up asleep on a warm tray of sunstone in a
> sunning yard at dawn, made of jittering bright strokes like scribbled light. A young woman with a lantern kneels
> beside it, smiling.
